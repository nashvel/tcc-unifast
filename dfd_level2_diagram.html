Figure 4.3: Data Flow Diagram (DFD) Level 2 — Decomposition of Process 3.0 (Grantee Onboarding & Identity Verification)

Identity-first ordering: 3.1 issues a scoped onboarding session with no credential; the account password is created only at 3.6, after the biometric match at 3.4 (or staff approval at 3.5). The vault PIN (3.7) is an opt-in setting, not a step in the funnel.

Revised (identity-first activation): 3.1 was “Validate Token & Initialize Account” and set the password; 3.6 was “Configure Document Vault Security PIN”. The credential moved from 3.1 to 3.6, so the biometric match now gates account ownership rather than document upload. 3.7 is new. Everything else is unchanged.
